# Freeform and mixed CAD

Use `colocad build MODEL.json NEW_DIRECTORY` with `format: "colocad/hybrid/1"` and mm. Locate the installed example at `node_modules/@coloopx/colocad/examples/hybrid-enclosure/model.json`. The full contract is exported at `@coloopx/colocad/hybrid/schema`.

- Establish compatible front, side and top silhouettes before detailing. Record uncertain depth and interface dimensions. Define the upper exterior and lower moving part independently. Give each spline station corresponding landmarks around cheek, crown and muzzle transitions. Check the actual silhouette after the loft.
- For an editable normal offset select one planar opening by world coordinate with `shell`, or build a closed `hollow`. For an art-directed interior model the second loft explicitly and use `cut`. Record that its wall varies and check wall probes on the resulting part.
- Keep an external skin as a closed oriented `import_mesh` solid and hardware as `import_step`. Declare STL/OBJ units. Reference relative paths and put colours on the instance under `material.color`. For a machineable interface use `cut` on B-reps or choose `mesh_cut` explicitly for a triangle result. `faceted_brep` exposes planar scan facets to exact operations.
- Build the real jaw and optics in each requested pose. Make the optical field from a cone at the sensor tip, aligned with its optical axis. Its end radius is range times tan(half the full field angle). Use the actual posed parts in checks. An intentional interpenetration must produce a positive result. A local wall probe crossing, slice or overhang report establishes only its measured quantity.
- Read `report.ok`, every check's method and volume, and any failed thickness span. The complete posed STEP path is `report.assemblyStep`. Request `facetedStep:true` to include meshes. Use the generated single-file `review.html` to compare projections and colours with the input. A manifold scan alone cannot establish a hidden cavity, a printer support strategy, or the dimensions of a lens mount.
